Data Deletion Instructions

If you signed in to the app with Threads

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  2. Find Vacancy Radar and remove it. The app immediately loses access to your account.
  3. To also delete the stored access token, open an issue (see below). We delete it within 30 days.

If your public Threads post was processed

The app keeps only the post ID, link and a one-way hash of the text for up to 60 days, after which they are deleted automatically. To have them deleted sooner, open an issue on GitHub with the link to your post. Do not include any other personal information. We complete requests within 30 days.

Як видалити дані

  1. Щоб забрати доступ: Threads → Налаштування → Акаунт → Дозволи вебсайтів → видаліть Vacancy Radar.
  2. Щоб видалити ID чи хеш вашого допису раніше ніж через 60 днів: створіть звернення на GitHub з посиланням на допис. Ми видалимо дані протягом 30 днів.
