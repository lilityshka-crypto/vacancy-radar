Vacancy Radar

Vacancy Radar is a personal job-search assistant for a graphic and AI designer based in Ukraine. It looks for fresh public Threads posts in which employers or clients are hiring a designer, and sends the relevant ones to its owner as private Telegram notifications.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with Threads once and grants the threads_basic and threads_keyword_search permissions.
  2. Every 15 minutes during the day, the app searches recent public Threads posts for job-related keywords, such as “looking for a designer” or “#vacancy”.
  3. Posts that look like job offers are evaluated for relevance to the owner’s skills.
  4. Relevant posts are sent to the owner’s private Telegram chat with a link back to the original Threads post, so the owner can read it and reply on Threads.

The app does not publish, like, reply or send messages on Threads. It is used only by its owner.

Радар вакансій

Особистий помічник для пошуку роботи у сфері графічного та AI-дизайну. Він шукає свіжі публічні дописи в Threads, де роботодавці чи замовники шукають дизайнера, і надсилає відповідні вакансії в особистий чат Telegram з посиланням на допис.

Застосунок нічого не публікує в Threads, не ставить вподобань і не пише повідомлень. Ним користується лише одна людина.
